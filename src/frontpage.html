Lead Story — Markets

Huang returns to Nvidia in a stunning reversal — and Wall Street erupts

SANTA CLARA — Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, 11 days after he resigned in scandal, bowing to pressure from President Donald Trump — who called into the board meeting — and from investors. Shares in the chipmaker surged more than 30 percent, the Nasdaq leapt 6.5 percent and the S&P 500 rose 4.7 percent, snapping Wall Street’s longest losing streak since April 2024. Lawmakers criticised the decision, and the New York Times reported that US intelligence had tracked alleged contacts between Alice Huang and China’s spy agency during her relationship with the chief executive.

War at Sea

Ukraine sinks a Russian warship off Libya

BENGHAZI — President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed that Ukrainian naval drones sank a Russian warship at the Libyan port of Benghazi and hit a second vessel — the war’s boldest strike yet in the Mediterranean. The UN mission in Libya urged an investigation; the Kremlin warned of escalation, and Albania denied the drones had been launched from its territory.

Ukraine War
